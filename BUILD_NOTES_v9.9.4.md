# Gallery v9.9.4 - World Placement Artwork Editor List

## Included
- Added a 3D / World Placements list to the Artwork Placement Editor.
- World-positioned records from half walls and partitions can now be selected and edited for title, artist, media, statement, sizing and tags.
- World-only records are no longer silently defaulted to `main/back` during editor data upgrade.
- Applying metadata changes to world-only records preserves their 3D coordinates.

## Notes
- Repositioning world-only artwork still belongs in 3D curator mode.
- Room-wall 3D placements continue to appear both in the world list and on their mapped wall canvas.
