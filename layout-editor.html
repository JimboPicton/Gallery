Layout Editor

Drag rooms on the floorplan. Hallway connection nodes are numbered on each room. Click "Add Hallway by Selecting Nodes", then click the starting node and destination node. Hallways are anchored to room wall connection points: one point on the short walls and two points on the long walls. Export the JSON and replace gallery-layout.json in your GitHub repo.
